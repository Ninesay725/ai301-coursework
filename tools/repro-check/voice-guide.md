# Voice guide: how I talk upstream

## Who I am in threads

I have backend experience with Python, Java, and TypeScript, including RAG projects.
I am a new contributor here. I can investigate a reported problem and share what I actually tested.

## Rules I write by

### Rule: Name the check

Say which behavior I will investigate and how I will start.

- Wrong: "I would love to contribute to this exciting project."
- Right: "I will try the reported empty-input case on the listed version."

### Rule: Promise work I control

Commit to investigating and reporting, without promising a fix or a date.

- Wrong: "I will fix this bug by Friday."
- Right: "I will investigate the failure and report my results."

### Rule: Keep the claim inside the evidence

Name the tested scope when stating a result.

- Wrong: "This issue is fixed now."
- Right: "I could not reproduce the failure on version 2.1 with this input."

### Rule: Use plain words

Use short paragraphs with concrete facts. Drop inflated introductions and jargon that adds no meaning.

- Wrong: "I leveraged a comprehensive validation methodology to ascertain the anomaly."
- Right: "I ran the example and got the same error."

## Things I never post

- Claims that I ran a command I have not run.
- Promises of a fix or delivery date before doing the work.
- Blame, demands for a quick reply, or "same here" without my own evidence.
